## 🌀 Continuous Improvement Frameworks

### 1. DMAIC vs. DMADV
* **DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control):** Used for **improving existing** ERP/CRM processes (e.g., reducing data entry errors in D365).
* **DMADV (Define, Measure, Analyze, Design, Verify):** Used for **designing new** Greenfield solutions (e.g., building a new automated reporting engine from scratch).

### 2. SIPOC Template (High-Level Process Overview)
