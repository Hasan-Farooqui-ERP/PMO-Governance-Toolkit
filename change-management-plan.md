# 🔄 Change Management & CCB Protocol

This protocol ensures that "Scope Creep" is mathematically managed and stakeholder-approved.

### 📝 Change Request (CR) Process
1. **Request:** Submitter identifies a required change in Scope, Time, or Budget.
2. **Impact Assessment:** PM (Hasan) assesses impact on Critical Path and **8/80 Capacity**.
3. **Review:** Change Control Board (CCB) reviews the "Change/No-Change" impact analysis.
4. **Approval:** Signed off before any engineering resources are diverted.
